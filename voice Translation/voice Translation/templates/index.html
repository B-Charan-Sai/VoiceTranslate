Mobile Permissions & HTTPS Guide

Why mobile browsers block microphones on HTTP and how to connect safely.

1

Secure Context Requirement

Modern mobile browsers (Chrome on Android, Safari on iOS) prohibit microphone access on plain http:// addresses for privacy. They require https:// or localhost.

2

Free Public HTTPS Tunnel

Run start_public_https.bat in the app folder to get a trusted https://...trycloudflare.com link that works with microphone permissions on any phone worldwide.

3

One-Click Cloud Deploy

Deploy for free on Render, Railway, or Hugging Face. The project includes ready-to-run render.yaml and Dockerfile configurations.
